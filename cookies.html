Cookies Policy

Last Updated: June 1, 2026

This document details our technical practices regarding how NamesOnWheel utilizes browser cookies, localized storage nodes, and minimal telemetry tokens to maintain a fluid, functional user experience across our web layout ecosystem.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are ultra-lightweight text strings securely placed inside your computer or mobile hardware's browser storage directory when you load digital interfaces. They serve as reliable local memory tags, enabling websites to remember your unique UI preferences and state parameters without re-querying raw inputs from scratch on every page load.

2. How NamesOnWheel Uses Cookies

Because Abraham designed this tool on a sleek client-side application layer, we purposefully avoid server-side data retention. Instead, we rely on client-side cookies and browser localStorage variables to execute foundational features:

  • Preference Retention: Keeping track of your customized settings, such as custom sound toggles, volume sliders, dark/light theme adjustments, and palette variations.
  • Input Persistence: Keeping your entered names list active inside the input pane so your data remains intact if you accidentally hit refresh.
  • Performance Optimization: Ensuring foundational canvas components and JavaScript files load instantly out of your localized cache directories rather than requesting them from the network repeatedly.

⚡ Privacy Assurance: None of our native application cookies extract raw personal credentials, identity strings, or external browsing histories from your physical device.

3. Third-Party Tracking & Advertising Cookies

To keep this randomization utility accessible, we may coordinate with programmatic advertising partners and analytical networks. These third parties deploy automated cookies to evaluate user session trends and serve contextual ad creative matching broad audience segments. These vendors utilize their own independent tracking architectures governed by their respective operational privacy standards.

4. Your Choice: Managing and Disabling Cookies

You have full sovereign authority over how your hardware stores tracking tokens. You can configure your internet browser to refuse cookies entirely, block third-party scripts, or flash an alert whenever a new cookie is introduced.

To fine-tune your parameters, head to your browser’s settings panel (usually under Privacy & Security or Clear Browsing Data). Please be aware that wiping your cookie cache completely will remove your saved lists and spin parameters, reverting the wheel tool back to its initial default state on your next visit.
