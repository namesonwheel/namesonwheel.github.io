About Us

The Story Behind the Wheel

Welcome to NamesOnWheel, your go-to digital destination for clean, unadulterated, and high-performance random selection routines. Whether you are dividing an engineering team into sprint pairs, running an online sweepstakes giveaway, or simply settling a friendly household debate on what to order for dinner, we believe decision-making should be fair, interactive, and completely frictionless.

Who We Are

NamesOnWheel was envisioned, architected, and fine-tuned by Abraham, a dedicated web developer and SEO expert. Built out of a sheer passion for crafting highly responsive client-side web applications, this platform aims to eliminate the sluggish clunkiness, aggressive cookie walls, and massive tracking payloads common to legacy decision utilities across the internet ecosystem.

Our Core Engineering Philosophy

As web professionals, we prioritize performance metrics and user privacy over everything else. That is why our choice engine relies on a strict architectural blueprint:

  • Zero Cloud Databases: Your entered lists, parameters, and items reside strictly within your active browser runtime instance space.
  • Search Optimization & Clean Code: Built on minimalist client-side rendering (CSR) logic, ensuring instant layout painting, crisp CSS performance, and seamless animations.
  • Absolute Transparency: No covert behavior profiling. We build software to help you pick a random name, not to catalog your online identity details.
